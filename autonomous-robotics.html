01 / Competition design

Building robots as integrated systems

Our team developed two robots for the 2021–2022 VEX AI Robotics Competition. Success depended on bringing together reliable mechanical systems and sensing hardware that could support autonomous operation.

02 / Mechanical build

From CAD to robot hardware

I focused mainly on mechanical design and construction, including the drivetrain, ball intake, and ball-launching mechanisms. I modeled parts in SolidWorks and fabricated custom components with a Prusa 3D printer, iterating designs to fit the robots’ physical constraints.

03 / Perception & integration

Connecting vision to the robot

I also worked with NVIDIA Jetson hardware, an Intel RealSense camera, and a FLIR camera, contributing code and integration work for the robots’ perception systems.

04 / Leadership

Coordinating the build

As team captain, I supported the team across mechanical and software workstreams, learning how to make subsystem decisions in the context of a working robot rather than as isolated components.
