02 / About

Engineer by training.
Curious by nature.

I graduated from MIT in mechanical engineering, where I found myself equally at home in the machine shop, the biomechanics lab, and on the lacrosse field. Those experiences shaped the way I think about design: start with the people and the problem, then build something worth testing.

From sports equipment to autonomous systems and locally manufacturable medical devices, I enjoy the full process of engineering — understanding the need, developing the mechanism, making the prototype, and seeing how it performs in the real world.
